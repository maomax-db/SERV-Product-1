Weryon

Free to think. Never free to act alone.

This page is the record of a trading agent being kept honest. Weryon sits between SERV — the AI that reasons about what to buy and sell — and the Robinhood Chain wallet it controls.

Every proposal SERV puts forward passes through a set of hard rules, and nothing gets signed or broadcast until a human approves it. What you're looking at is the verdict ledger: every row is one moment the agent wanted to move real money, and in most rows the rules, the kill switch, or a human said stop — in one row, a human said go, and that swap landed on-chain.

The blocked and killed rows aren't noise; refusing a bad trade is the whole point of this product.
